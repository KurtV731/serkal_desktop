Private test build 0.0.5a15 · 17 October 2026
Companion desktop build: Alpha 22.1 (displayed as Alpha 221)

Please read this before testing. It replaces the short installation note in
your package and describes a15, not features planned for later builds.

1. WHAT IS INCLUDED
SerKal is a series-calendar entry tool and a personal series archive. Search
TMDB, view posters and summaries, add seasons to your archive, and synchronise
their dates with Google Calendar. Notes, date offsets, watched markers and
complete-series deletion are supported. It does not play or download films
or episodes, and it cannot tell what you have actually watched.

Your test package contains:
- serkal-desktop-alpha-22-1-test.exe: Windows installer;
- serkal-android-0.0.5a15.apk: Android app;
- matching .sha256 files: optional integrity checks, not installers.
Outside the package, the APK may be named 0.0.5a15-serkal-android.apk instead.
The names refer to the same approved file only if its checksum matches.

2. INSTALLING OR UPDATING
Install and check Windows first. Answer any open SerKal prompts, such as a
save confirmation, and close SerKal completely before updating it.
Android requires a phone or tablet running Android 7.0 or later. Testing has
covered a Pixel 8 and a Samsung tablet, not every device. APK files do not
run on an iPhone or iPad.

Extract the ZIP first. Transfer the APK to your Android device and open it
there, for example from Downloads. Do not open the Windows EXE on your phone.
If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for the specific browser or file
manager opening this APK. You can remove that permission afterwards. Wording
varies by device. Play Protect may offer a scan or warn about an unknown app.
Keep Play Protect enabled. If it reports actual malware or blocks installation
in an unclear way, stop and send Kurt the exact message; do not disable
protection across the device.

Do NOT uninstall an existing SerKal app first. Install a15 over it as an update.
Android checks the package identity and signing certificate. If the identical
a15 is already installed, “Open” may appear straight away. Uninstalling can
remove local data and settings, including changes not yet uploaded. If you see
“App not installed”, report it rather than trying an uninstall as a workaround.

The app must display version 0.0.5a15. It runs in landscape orientation.

3. SETTING UP GOOGLE AND TMDB
Use your OWN Google account. It need not have a Gmail address, but it must be
a Google account. Choose the same account on your own SerKal devices. Each
tester uses their own account: sharing between your devices does not create
a public archive shared with other testers.

SerKal may try to connect to Google automatically at startup. Tap “Google” to
reconnect or choose an account. You can select “Cancel” before “Continue”.
Use Android Back to leave Google's account/consent screen. While SerKal itself
is waiting for a connection, tapping “Google” again offers cancellation.
Cancelling preserves an existing local archive. The status may then show a
cancelled connection or a pending transfer.

Enter Google credentials only in Google's own sign-in screen. The APK contains
no personal Google tokens or developer account. SerKal requests access to its
private Google Drive app data and its SerKal calendar; the calendar list lets
it find the target calendar.

A TMDB key already shared through your account is adopted automatically. If
none exists, paste your own TMDB credential using “API key”. Do not type a long
key character by character. If asked to “Share”, this means making the key
available to your other SerKal installations under the same Google account,
not publishing it. Other testers do not automatically receive your key.

Already have a desktop archive? Start the current desktop build on the previous
source PC first and wait for its full Google synchronisation to finish. Then
connect/synchronise Android. “First transfer … previous source PC” means that
migration has not finished. Once Android has received the full copy, your PC
does not have to stay on. A genuinely new account with no legacy archive can
start an empty archive on Android, but still needs a working TMDB credential.

4. FINDING YOUR WAY AROUND
Search on the left, view/edit in the middle, archive on the right.
- DE/EN at the top switches language. The button names the destination language.
- Light/Dark changes the main view. Some Android dialogs remain light in a15.
- + and − change magnification; the percentage button resets to 100%.
  Pinch and pan are also supported. If a tablet ignores the initial gesture
  at 100%, try + once first.
- Date ↓ / A–Z switches archive ordering. Date uses the active season start,
  newest first; entries without a date come first.
- Archive “Menu”: Sync now, Reload archive, Maintenance, Last maintenance
  results and Log.
- Tap an archive entry for details and its poster. Providers appear below the
  synopsis. A missing poster does not by itself mean an archive/calendar failure.

5. ADDING AND EDITING A SERIES
Enter a title, select the correct search result and check its details.
Tap “Add to archive”, enter a season number, choose “Prepare” and check the
summary. “Send” confirms a local save, not a completed Google upload. In a15,
all episode dates must be available before the season can be added. Incomplete
episode dates cause the new entry to be rejected rather than partly inserted.
TMDB dates are not automatically Australian or German streaming dates. Seasons
whose dates are entirely before the current calendar year are not added as
new regular calendar events.

Select an existing archive entry, then “Edit”:
- Edit notes / offset / provider, then “Send” to save. A separate line
  “OffsetDE: +1D” moves dates one day later relative to the ORIGINAL dates;
  “OffsetDE: -1D” moves them one day earlier. +1D followed by -1D does not
  return to the original dates. Remove the offset line to reset that offset,
  retaining your other notes. This field keeps its OffsetDE name in English.
- Override a provider with a separate line such as “Provider: Paramount+”
  (the German “Anbieter:” is also accepted). Remove that line to return to
  the automatically reported provider information.
- Toggle watched marks a season as watched or reverses your choice.
- Delete complete series removes ALL its archived seasons and its managed
  calendar events after a successful sync. Read the confirmation carefully.
  Only use a deliberately added test entry for a deletion test. Permanent
  announcement cards created by the desktop intentionally remain.

Archive symbols on the right:
Green tick = explicitly marked watched by you. Red tick = season dates have
passed, but it is not marked watched. Yellow clock = upcoming start; blue dot
= season running according to its dates; circle = no dates available.
Tap a red/green tick to toggle it, or use “Edit”. No viewing is detected for you.

6. SAVING AND SWITCHING DEVICES
Each device has a full local archive copy. Edits save locally, then transfer
in the background. Continuous cloud polling is deliberately not used: devices
sync at startup, after edits, or on request. A second device left open will
not necessarily notice another device's edit immediately.

“Saved locally / transfer pending” confirms only this device.
“Transferring” means it is still working; you can continue using the interface.
“Saved on Google” confirms that this archive state is available to other devices.
Tap the shortened status below the archive to read its full text. Wait for
Google confirmation before switching devices. Restart the second device's app
or use Menu → Sync now. “Reload archive” refreshes the view through the current
connection and initiates a sync; “Sync now” is the direct transfer action.

Internet is needed for the first transfer and synchronisation. A previously
loaded local copy may remain usable after a connection failure; fresh searches
and posters need internet. After switching apps or Android terminating SerKal,
reopen and sync if necessary. Uploads are not guaranteed while the app is
completely stopped.

Avoid deliberately editing on several devices at once. Conflicts protect
copies rather than silently merging contradictory changes. Low storage can
make the archive read-only: at least 10 MiB reserve is required, and larger
archives may need more. Read the synchronisation status.

7. MAINTENANCE AND STREAMING PROVIDERS
Menu → Maintenance. Select your real streaming country, e.g. AU for Australia
or DE for Germany. Switching the interface to English does NOT automatically
select AU. Maintenance checks dates/providers, updates eligible existing
entries and lists new seasons as suggestions. It does not automatically add
further seasons. It starts at your first archived regular season: with S07 and
S09, S08 matters, while S01–S06 are ignored. Title metadata may also be updated.

You can reopen the last results. “NOT APPLIED” or an incomplete check does not
mean full success. Avoid further archive edits during maintenance; if applying
the result is rejected because the archive changed, repeat the check later.

Provider information comes from JustWatch via TMDB. It concerns the selected
country and the series as a whole, not guaranteed availability of every season
or episode. No reported offers does not mean “unavailable everywhere”.
Subscriptions, rental/purchase terms and availability can change; check with
the service before paying.

8. CURRENT a15 LIMITS
- Android maintenance does not yet create the new desktop announcement cards,
  permanent date-change history or (?) markers in the archive itself.
- “Missing season” can still refer to a season that has only been announced.
- The newest language-safe desktop title correction is not fully ported to
  Android. Please report foreign-language/wrong titles with a concrete example.
- Provider descriptions written by Android into Google Calendar can still be
  German, even with the interface set to English.
- Some dialogs do not follow Dark mode yet. Small text, gestures and differences
  between devices are explicitly part of this test.
- A freely editable series-title field and interactive maintenance questions
  are not promised features of this APK.

9. SHORT TEST RUN
1) Check a15, landscape layout, language, Light/Dark and +/−.
2) Connect your own Google account; check key adoption and archive contents.
3) With a series selected, test Google → Cancel: archive/selection should remain.
   Optionally choose Continue, then Back in Google's dialog.
4) Open an archive entry: check poster, synopsis, providers and Date/A–Z.
5) Add an unarchived test series with complete dates. Observe local save, then
   wait for “Saved on Google”.
6) Sync another device of your own: the same entry should appear there.
7) Toggle watched both ways and add a harmless note; sync again.
8) Run maintenance for the correct country and reopen its last results.
9) Delete only your newly added test series, confirm, wait for Google sync and
   check the second device.
You can take part with one Android device; simply skip the two-device step.

10. YOUR DATA AND BUG REPORTS
Archive data and a shared TMDB key are held locally and/or in your Google
account's private app data (not a normal folder in My Drive); managed events
are held in your Google calendar.
TMDB receives search, metadata and image requests. SerKal does not download
films, monitor your television or infer your viewing. Listing calendars is not
unrestricted permission to read all calendar contents. This technical guide
does not replace the privacy notice on serkal.de.

Report device/Android version, app version, language, steps, time and the exact
message to Kurt; include Menu → Log where useful. Check screenshots/logs for
personal data before sharing. Never post passwords, TMDB keys, access tokens
or full archives publicly. Report conflicts, blocked syncs or manual calendar
changes instead of deleting tokens, calendars, archives or app data as a fix.
Manual Google Calendar changes can pause an automatic sync.
